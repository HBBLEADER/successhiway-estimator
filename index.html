Not there yet within 3 months — what to adjust

    This is a projection tool, not an income guarantee. Your goal is evaluated as a MONTHLY income figure — each simulated month's own earnings (not a running total added up across months) is what's checked against your goal, matching how the compensation plan actually resets and pays out each month. This projection runs a maximum of 3 months. That limit is intentional: Coded Bonus pays to infinite depth with no generational limit, so its growth compounds very quickly as a team builds — which is a real, accurate feature of the compensation plan, not a flaw in this tool. Capping the projection at 3 months keeps the numbers meaningful and avoids projecting so far out that the math stops being a realistic reflection of early team-building activity. It's also where most Runners realistically hit their first goal. This tool models a grounded, real-world breakdown: a small, explicitly-placed handful of Leaders that Follow You — personally recruiting at your pace indefinitely (real teams are usually built by a handful of leaders like this, not by everyone duplicating equally), plus everyone else you personally enroll split into a group who order at Gold and personally recruit once before stopping, and a group who order at Silver and personally recruit twice (or never) before stopping. Real teams vary widely based on individual effort. This tool models only your own personal activity and the activity of your personally-enrolled downline — it does not model spillover volume from upline sponsoring efforts anywhere, including Matrix Pay, since company-wide spillover can't be predicted for an individual projection. No income claims or guarantees are made or implied. Please refer to the official GreatLife Compensation Plan for complete details.
