Not there yet within 3 months — what to adjust

    This is a projection tool, not an income guarantee. Your goal is evaluated as a MONTHLY income figure — each simulated month's own earnings (not a running total added up across months) is what's checked against your goal, matching how the compensation plan actually resets and pays out each month. This projection runs a maximum of 3 months. That limit is intentional: Coded Bonus pays to infinite depth with no generational limit, so its growth compounds very quickly as a team builds — which is a real, accurate feature of the compensation plan, not a flaw in this tool. Capping the projection at 3 months keeps the numbers meaningful and avoids projecting so far out that the math stops being a realistic reflection of early team-building activity. It's also where most Runners realistically hit their first goal. "Full Follow Sponsor" models a "Do The Same Thing" (DTST) scenario where every team member follows the same activity pattern you enter above, indefinitely. "Realistic Mix" models a more grounded three-tier breakdown instead: a smaller group who build like you and keep recruiting, a larger group who order at Gold and personally recruit once before stopping, and the rest who order at Silver and personally recruit twice before stopping. Either way, real teams vary widely based on individual effort. This tool models only your own personal activity and the activity of your personally-enrolled downline — it does not model spillover volume from upline sponsoring efforts anywhere, including Matrix Pay, since company-wide spillover can't be predicted for an individual projection. No income claims or guarantees are made or implied. Please refer to the official GreatLife Compensation Plan for complete details.
