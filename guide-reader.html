How to Use This Guide

▲ Contents

This guide walks through two complete sample runs of the GreatLife Worldwide Business Income Estimator — a free planning tool built for GreatLife distributors and their prospects. It shows exactly what a Runner enters, and exactly what the compensation plan pays back, week by week and month by month, based on those entries.

The Estimator models a pure duplication business: every person the Runner personally enrolls goes on to recruit at that same weekly pace, and everyone's autoship auto-upgrades on the same schedule as the Runner's own (1 product → 2 products at 5 personal enrolled → 3 products at 9 personal enrolled). Nothing here is automatic or guaranteed; every result in these sample runs flows directly from the goal and the weekly recruiting pace entered.

Weekly vs. Monthly: Why the Split Matters

GreatLife pays five income streams on two different schedules. Fast Start Bonus and Weekly Coded Bonus are paid weekly, on real enrollment activity that happened that week. Matrix Pay, Monthly Coded Bonus, and Lifestyle Bonus are paid monthly, based on current order/reorder volume. This guide keeps those two schedules visually separate — the Week-by-Week table only ever shows what's actually paid that week, and the Monthly Income Breakdown shows the monthly streams and the true month total, split out by month.

Why Two Sample Runs?

Sample Run 1 sets a modest, believable monthly goal ($1,000) at a steady pace (3 new reps/week) — and shows how quickly even a conservative goal can be reached once a small team starts duplicating. Sample Run 2 sets an ambitious goal ($10,000/month) at a much slower pace (just 1 new rep/week) — and shows that the compensation plan's unlimited-depth Coded Bonus can still get a Runner there surprisingly fast, because the whole downline is compounding at that same pace, not just the Runner alone.

Purpose

To help people picture, in concrete numbers, what consistent weekly effort in this business can realistically produce — and to help a sponsor guide a new distributor toward a goal that's neither too small nor too large.

Ultimate goal: to help people achieve great health and wealth.

The Five Income Streams Modeled

  1. Fast Start Bonus (weekly) — $20/product paid to the enroller on direct-enrollment orders.
  2. Weekly Coded Bonus (weekly) — enrollment-triggered, unlimited depth, starts at the Runner's 3rd personal enrollment (Silver).
  3. Monthly Coded Bonus (monthly) — order/reorder-triggered, unlimited depth.
  4. Matrix Pay (monthly) — 2x14 matrix, dual-gated by the Runner's own autoship level and personal sponsor count. Levels 1–7 require only 1-product autoship — no personal enrollment minimum.
  5. Lifestyle Bonus (monthly) — GPV-tiered bonus, requires Platinum rank + qualifying volume.

Not modeled: Retail Commissions, One-Time Achievement Bonuses, spillover, or the $14.95 Affiliate tier.

Disclaimer & Purpose Statement

▲ Contents

This is a projection tool, not an income guarantee.

The Week-by-Week Progress table in this guide shows only what's actually paid on a weekly basis — Fast Start Bonus and Weekly Coded Bonus. Matrix Pay, Monthly Coded Bonus, and Lifestyle Bonus are monthly streams, evaluated as a current run-rate snapshot and shown in the Monthly Income Breakdown for each month, not blended into the weekly figures. This projection runs a maximum of 6 months (a 3-month soft target, 6-month hard stop). That limit is intentional: Coded Bonus pays to infinite depth with no generational limit, so its growth compounds very quickly as a team builds — a real, accurate feature of the compensation plan, not a flaw in this tool. Capping the projection keeps the numbers meaningful and avoids projecting so far out that the math stops being a realistic reflection of early team-building activity.

This tool models a pure duplication business: every person the Runner personally enrolls goes on to recruit new people at that Runner's own entered weekly pace, indefinitely, and everyone's autoship auto-upgrades on the same 1→2→3 product schedule as the Runner's own. There is no manually-entered "Leaders" count and no percentage-mix of behavior groups — every enrollee is assumed to build exactly like the Runner does.

Real teams vary widely based on individual effort. This tool models only the Runner's own personal activity and the activity of their personally-enrolled downline duplicating that same activity — it does not model spillover volume from upline sponsoring efforts anywhere, including Matrix Pay, since company-wide spillover can't be predicted for an individual projection.

No income claims or guarantees are made or implied. Please refer to the official GreatLife Compensation Plan for complete details.
